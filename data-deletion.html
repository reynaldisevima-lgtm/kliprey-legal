Data Deletion Instructions

Kliprey Uploader · Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Home

Kliprey Uploader stores account data only on its operator's own computer: the account ID, name, profile picture and encrypted access token of each connected YouTube, TikTok, Facebook Page or Instagram account, and the status of videos the App uploaded. You can delete this data in any of the ways below.

1. In the App

Open Channel, choose the account, click Atur (Settings), then Hapus akun (Remove account). The account's token and profile data are deleted immediately, and its queued uploads are cancelled.

2. On the platform (revokes access)

  • Facebook & Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Kliprey Uploader → Remove. On Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Remove.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Kliprey → Remove access.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Kliprey → Remove.

After access is revoked, the stored token stops working and the App marks the account as disconnected.

3. By email

Send an email to reynaldisevima@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account name or ID. All stored data for that account is deleted within 7 days, and the deletion is confirmed by reply.
