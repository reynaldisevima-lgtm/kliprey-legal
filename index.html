Kliprey Uploader

A personal desktop tool that publishes the owner's short videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Kliprey Uploader runs on its owner's Windows computer. It is used by a single creator to prepare short vertical videos and publish them to the creator's own channels — creyative_id, aldictive_id and creyzytivity — on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. It is not offered, sold or distributed to other users.

How it works with TikTok

  1. Connect. On the Channel page the owner clicks "Connect TikTok" and authorizes with TikTok Login Kit. Only the basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) is read, to show which account is connected.
  2. Review. The owner opens a finished video and reviews its title, caption and hashtags.
  3. Upload as draft. The video is sent with the Content Posting API to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. Publish in TikTok. The creator edits and posts the draft in the TikTok app, so nothing is published without the creator's final review.

Data

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer only. No data is shared, sold or used for advertising. Accounts can be disconnected at any time. Details: Privacy Policy.
