Kliprey Uploader

A personal desktop tool that prepares short vertical videos and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts.

What Kliprey Uploader does

Kliprey Uploader runs on its owner's Windows computer. It is used by one creator to manage the creator's own channels: creyative_id, aldictive_id and creyzytivity. It is not offered, sold or distributed to other people, and it has no public sign-up.

✂️ Prepare videos

Cuts highlights from the creator's own or licensed long videos, adds subtitles, titles and branding, and renders vertical 9:16 clips.

📝 Write captions

Suggests a title, caption and hashtags for each clip. The creator reviews and edits them before anything is posted.

🗓️ Schedule

Queues each clip to the creator's chosen accounts at the posting times the creator sets.

📤 Publish

Uploads the clip through each platform's official API and shows the result: published, draft, or failed with the reason.

How each platform is used

  1. Connect. On the Channel page the creator clicks "Connect" for a platform and signs in on that platform's own login page (OAuth). The App never sees the password.
  2. Review. The creator opens a finished clip and checks its title, caption, hashtags and privacy setting.
  3. Upload. The App sends the video to the platform's official upload endpoint, to the account the creator selected.
  4. Check. The App reads the processing status of that upload, so the creator sees whether it is live.

Your data

  • Everything stays on the creator's own computer. There is no Kliprey server or cloud copy.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI).
  • The App only uploads videos the creator chose. It does not read messages, followers, comments or other content.
  • No platform data is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • Accounts can be disconnected at any time, in the App or in the platform's settings. How to delete your data.
