Privacy Policy

Kliprey Uploader · Effective date: 31 October 2026

Kliprey Uploader ("the App") is a private, single-user tool run by an individual creator on their own computer. It prepares short videos and publishes them to the creator's own social media accounts (the channels creyative_id, aldictive_id and creyzytivity). The App is not offered to the public, has no user sign-up, and is only used to manage accounts that belong to its operator.

1. What data the App accesses

When the operator connects an account, the App receives only what the platform shares for the permissions requested:

PlatformPermissionsData received
TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API)user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish Account open ID, display name, avatar URL, access and refresh tokens, status of videos the App uploads.
YouTube (YouTube Data API)youtube.upload, youtube.readonly Channel ID, channel name and avatar, access and refresh tokens, processing status of videos the App uploads.
Facebook (Pages)pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts Page ID, Page name and picture, Page access token, status of Reels the App publishes.

The App does not read private messages, contacts, followers' personal data, comments, payment data or any content other than the videos it uploads itself.

2. How the data is used

  • To upload and publish videos, chosen by the operator, to the operator's own accounts.
  • To show the operator which account is connected and whether an upload succeeded.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics sold to others, or training of AI models.

3. Storage and security

  • All data stays on the operator's own computer, in a local database. There is no App server or cloud copy.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so only the operator's Windows user account can decrypt them.
  • Connections to the platforms use HTTPS.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share platform data with anyone. Data is only sent back to the platform it came from (for example a video sent to TikTok's upload endpoint). To produce narration or text for videos the operator may use AI and text-to-speech services; only the operator's own script text is sent there, never data received from TikTok, YouTube or Facebook.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Account data and tokens are kept only while the account is connected.
  • Removing an account in the App ("Hapus akun") deletes its stored tokens and profile data immediately.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time on the platform: TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Google → myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook → Settings → Business integrations.
  • Data deletion request: email the contact below with the subject "Data deletion"; all stored data for that account is deleted within 7 days and the deletion is confirmed by reply.

6. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is only used by its adult operator.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
